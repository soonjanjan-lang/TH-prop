Abstract

I am an economist at the UUM College of Business, where I coordinate BEER3043 Research Methodology for the Bachelor of Science in Economics (Hons). The course takes students from the philosophy of research and the scientific method, through data collection, fieldwork ethics, sampling and statistical testing, to a research proposal written in the standard format.

My aim is graduates who can plan, analyse, evaluate and communicate research on their own. Add one or two sentences on your own research questions and findings.
