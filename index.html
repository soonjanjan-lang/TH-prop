About

I am an economist at the UUM College of Business, where I coordinate BEER3043 Research Methodology for the Bachelor of Science in Economics (Hons). My teaching covers the philosophy of research, the scientific method, data collection and fieldwork ethics, sampling, statistical testing, and computer-based data analysis.

I care about helping students build the skills to plan, analyse, evaluate and communicate research, and about producing graduates with strong analytical ability and sound professional character. [Edit: add a sentence about your own research focus.]
