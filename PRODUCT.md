# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, vanilla CSS and vanilla JavaScript (user requirement). No framework, no build step. Hosted from the GitHub repo `soonjanjan-lang/TH-prop` (GitHub Pages-ready).

## Users

Academic peers: researchers, potential collaborators, conference organisers and journal editors who want to quickly understand Soon Jan Jan's expertise, record and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal CV landing page for Soon Jan Jan, an economist at the School of Economics, Finance and Banking, UUM College of Business, Universiti Utara Malaysia. Success: a peer finds the research focus, publications and contact route within seconds and leaves with a credible impression.

## Positioning

Coordinator of BEER3043 Research Methodology: an economist whose work centres on the rigour of the research process itself (theory, sampling, statistical testing, primary-data fieldwork, proposal writing).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: introduction, experience and education, teaching, publications (filterable), skills/methods, contact.
- "Download CV" prints the page to PDF; the page must print cleanly.
- Contact form opens the visitor's own mail client (no backend).
- Light and dark themes; must work at phone width.

## Evidence on Hand

- `A232 KP syllabus.docx`: the BEER3043 Research Methodology syllabus (semester A232), listing Soon Jan Jan as Course Coordinator with Raji Jimoh Olajide and Shri Dewi a/p Applanaidu.
- Absent and must not be fabricated: job title, degrees, research interests, publication list, metrics (years, citations, grants, student counts), email, profile links. These stay as visibly marked `[Edit]` placeholders until the user supplies them.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility over flourish: nothing on the page may overstate the record.
2. Scannable for a busy peer: research focus, publications and contact are reachable instantly.
3. The page doubles as a printable CV.
4. Placeholders are honest and easy to find (`[Edit]`).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both themes; respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; keyboard-navigable.
